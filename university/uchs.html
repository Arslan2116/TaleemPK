About UCHS

University of Child Health Sciences, Lahore (UCHS) is a public university in Lahore, recognised by the Higher Education Commission (HEC) of Pakistan, established in 2021.

Pakistan's first and only public sector pediatric university, established under the University of Child Health Sciences Lahore Act 2021. Affiliated with The Children's Hospital, Lahore — one of the largest pediatric hospitals globally with 1,190 beds. Operates through three colleges: College of Paediatrics, College of Paediatric Nursing, and College of Allied Health Sciences.

  • Pakistan's first pediatric university
  • Chartered by Government of Punjab (2021)
  • Affiliated with The Children's Hospital Lahore (1,190 beds)
  • Three constituent colleges: Paediatrics, Nursing, Allied Health Sciences
  • Scholarships for deserving students
