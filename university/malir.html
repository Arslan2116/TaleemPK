About Malir

Malir University of Science and Technology is a private university in Karachi, recognized by the Higher Education Commission (HEC) of Pakistan, established in 2022.

Merit / Eligibility
Minimum 50% marks in Intermediate (HSSC) or equivalent (A-Levels, etc.) from a recognized board. Foreign qualifications require IBCC equivalency certificate. Shortlisted candidates called for interview. For BS Psychology: Intermediate in any discipline with at least 50% marks; age limit 17-35 years.
