About UMS

The University of Modern Sciences is a private university in Tando Muhammad Khan, recognized by the Higher Education Commission (HEC) of Pakistan, established in 2018.

Merit / Eligibility
5-Year Programs (MBBS, DPT, Pharm.D): Minimum 60% marks in Pre-Medical or equivalent. 4-Year Programs (BBA, BS IT, BS MLT, BS RIT, BSN): Minimum 50% marks in Pre-Medical/Pre-Engineering or equivalent. Postgraduate (MS/M.Phil): 4-year BS degree or equivalent 16 years of education in a relevant field.

Entry Test
Merit-based

Scholarships
Merit-based and need-based scholarships available. 50% discount on all programs (merit & need-based). Undergraduates must maintain minimum cumulative GPA of 3.0 to retain merit scholarship. Limited seats apply. Female students strongly encouraged to apply.
