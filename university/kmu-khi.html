About KMU-KHI

Karachi Metropolitan University (KMU-KHI) is a public university in Karachi, recognised by the Higher Education Commission (HEC) of Pakistan, established in 2023.

Public sector university established under Sindh Assembly Act No. LIV of 2023, comprising Karachi Medical and Dental College (KMDC) and Abbasi Shaheed Hospital as its constituent units. Located in Block M, North Nazimabad, Karachi. Recognized by HEC and committed to delivering quality education with an entrepreneurial and technology-driven approach.

  • Comprises KMDC and Abbasi Shaheed Hospital
