About Isra

Isra University (Isra) is a private university in Hyderabad, recognised by the Higher Education Commission (HEC) of Pakistan, established in 1997.

Isra University Hyderabad is a private medical university established in 1997. It operates a modern teaching hospital in Hyderabad and provides quality medical education to students from Sindh and Balochistan.

Isra Fee Structure 2026

The fee at Isra is approximately Rs. 80–150K/sem. Fees are revised each intake, so confirm the current figure with the university before you apply.

Merit & Admission Criteria

Merit / eligibility: 75%+ aggregate (MDCAT)

Entry test: MDCAT / Isra Test

Merit closes at a different level every year, depending on how many apply and how the test goes. Treat last year's figures as a guide, not a threshold.
