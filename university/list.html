About LIST

Lahore Institute of Science and Technology is a private university in Lahore, recognized by the Higher Education Commission (HEC) of Pakistan, established in 2022.

Quick Facts

💰
Fee / Semester
Rs. 110,000–130,000/sem (varies by program)
🎯
Merit / Eligibility
UG: Minimum 50% marks in Intermediate/FA/FSc/ICS/I.Com/DAE or equivalent. BS (SE), BS Engineering Technologies (Electrical/Civil), DPT: Minimum 60% marks. Postgraduate: Bachelor's in second division (min 45% or CGPA 2.0/4.0) + entry test + interview. Age limit: BS max 22 years; Master/MBA max 28 years; MBA Executive max 45 years.

Entry Test
Institute Admission Test + Interview
