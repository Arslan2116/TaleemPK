About KASBIT

Khadim Ali Shah Bukhari Institute of Technology (KASBIT) is a private university in Karachi, recognised by the Higher Education Commission (HEC) of Pakistan, established in 1999.

Pakistan's first private sector institute of higher education registered as a corporate body, established in Karachi in 1999. Chartered by the Government of Sindh and HEC-recognized with the highest W(4) category rating. KASBIT is a member of AACSB and was the first ISO-9001 certified degree-awarding institute in Pakistan's private sector.

  • First private sector institute registered as a corporate body in Pakistan
  • HEC recognized with highest W(4) category rating
  • Member of AACSB (global business school accreditation)
  • First ISO-9001 Certified Degree Awarding Institute in Pakistan's private sector
  • Three campus locations in Karachi
