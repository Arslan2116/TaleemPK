About AGU

Al-Ghazali University (AGU) is a private university in Karachi, recognised by the Higher Education Commission (HEC) of Pakistan.

Private university in Karachi offering a unique blend of traditional Islamic education and contemporary academic programs. Recognized by HEC. Combines practical skills, technology, and academic excellence with strong Islamic values across Computer Science, Shari'ah & Finance, and Islamic Studies programs.

  • HEC recognized university
  • Unique blend of Islamic and contemporary education
  • BS Computer Science and BS Shari'ah & Finance programs
  • MS Islamic Banking & Finance
  • Need-based, merit-based, and interest-free loan assistance
