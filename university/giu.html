About GIU

Green International University, Lahore (GIU) is a private university in Lahore, recognised by the Higher Education Commission (HEC) of Pakistan, established in 2020.

Private university chartered by the Government of Punjab under The Green International University Act 2020 (Act VI of 2020). Established by Madinah Foundation and HEC recognized, located in Bhobtian, Lahore. Offers programs across 10 faculties including Computing, Pharmacy, Business, Social Sciences, Engineering, and Arts.

  • Chartered by Government of Punjab (Act VI of 2020)
  • HEC recognized university
  • Established by Madinah Foundation
  • 10 faculties with diverse program offerings
  • 12 residential halls for ~1,400 students
  • Merit & need-based scholarships up to 100%

Merit & Admission Criteria

Merit / eligibility: Based on previous academic record. Merit scholarships: 15% waiver for 90%+ aggregate, 10% for 85–89.9%.

Entry test: Merit-based / Admission Test (where applicable)

Merit closes at a different level every year, depending on how many apply and how the test goes. Treat last year's figures as a guide, not a threshold.
