About GCU Lahore

Government College University Lahore (GCU Lahore) is a public university in Lahore, recognised by the Higher Education Commission (HEC) of Pakistan, established in 1864.

GC University Lahore is one of Pakistan's oldest and most prestigious public universities (est. 1864 as Government College). Its iconic Katchery Road campus is the alma mater of Nobel laureate Dr. Abdus Salam and many of Pakistan's leading writers, scientists and statesmen.

  • Established 1864 — Pakistan's historic institution
  • Alma mater of Dr. Abdus Salam (Nobel laureate)
  • Iconic Lahore campus & literary heritage
  • 349,797+ books in library
  • Strong arts, sciences & languages

Merit & Admission Criteria

Merit / eligibility: 60% HSSC/Intermediate + 40% GCU Entry Test. Minimum 50% in Intermediate required. English Literature & Political Science: written test + interview.

Entry test: GCU Entry Test (GAT for MS/MPhil)

Seats: about ~5,000/year.

Merit closes at a different level every year, depending on how many apply and how the test goes. Treat last year's figures as a guide, not a threshold.
