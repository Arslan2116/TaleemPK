About VU

Virtual University of Pakistan is a public university in Online, recognized by the Higher Education Commission (HEC) of Pakistan, established in 2002.

Quick Facts

💰
Fee / Semester
Rs. 1,000–2,950/credit hour
🎯
Merit / Eligibility
Admission is merit-based, considering the previous academic record. For MS/MPhil programs, a valid GRE/HAT General test score is mandatory. Admissions are offered twice a year (Spring and Fall).
📝
Entry Test
Merit-based / GRE/HAT (for MS/MPhil)
👥
Seats
Open enrollment (online)
🎓
Scholarships
Merit-based (CGPA 3.5+), need-based, special category (orphans, disabled students), and sports scholarships. The Chief Minister Punjab Honhaar Scholarship Program covers 100% tuition for eligible students.
🏠
Hostel
As a primarily online university, VU does not offer on-campus hostel facilities. Private hostels are available near some campuses.
