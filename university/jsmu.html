About JSMU

Jinnah Sindh Medical University (JSMU) is a public university in Karachi, recognised by the Higher Education Commission (HEC) of Pakistan, established in 1973.

Jinnah Sindh Medical University (JSMU) Karachi, formerly Dow Medical College Karachi campus, is one of Sindh's leading public medical universities. It is affiliated with multiple teaching hospitals across Karachi.
