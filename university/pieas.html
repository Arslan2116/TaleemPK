About PIEAS

Pakistan Institute of Engineering and Applied Sciences (PIEAS) is a public university in Islamabad, recognised by the Higher Education Commission (HEC) of Pakistan, established in 1997.

PIEAS is a premier public research university managed by the Pakistan Atomic Energy Commission (PAEC), renowned for specialized engineering, nuclear research, and its prestigious PAEC Fellowship.

  • Managed by PAEC
  • HEC top-ranked Engineering university
  • PAEC Fellowship — free fee + stipend (MS)
  • Strong R&D & strategic-org links
💰

PIEAS Fee Structure 2026

The fee at PIEAS is approximately Rs. 1.3–1.6L/sem. Fees are revised each intake, so confirm the current figure with the university before you apply.

Merit & Admission Criteria

Merit / eligibility: 60% PIEAS Written Test + 40% FSc/A-Level (min 60% marks). MS: written test + interview (esp. for PAEC Fellowship).

Entry test: PIEAS Entry Test

Seats: about ~200/year.

Merit closes at a different level every year, depending on how many apply and how the test goes. Treat last year's figures as a guide, not a threshold.
