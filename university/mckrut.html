About MCKRUT

Mir Chakar Khan Rind University of Technology (MCKRUT) is a public university in Dera Ghazi Khan, recognised by the Higher Education Commission (HEC) of Pakistan, established in 2019.

Mir Chakar Khan Rind University of Technology (MCKRUT) is a public university located in Dera Ghazi Khan District, Punjab. It is the only technology university in Dera Ghazi Khan District, established in 2019. Recognized by the Higher Education Commission (HEC) and accredited by the National Technology Council (NTC).

  • Public sector university chartered by Government of Punjab
  • HEC recognized & NTC accredited
  • Only technology university in Dera Ghazi Khan District
  • Established in 2019
  • Offers BS and DAE programs in engineering and technology
  • Heavily subsidized tuition models for low-income students
