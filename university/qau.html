About QAU

Quaid-i-Azam University is a public university in Islamabad, recognized by the Higher Education Commission (HEC) of Pakistan, established in 1967.

Quick Facts

Fee / Semester
Rs. 60–80K/sem
🎯
Merit / Eligibility
Merit on academic record (≈30% Matric + 70% HSSC) with strict provincial/regional quotas. LLB needs LAT; MPhil/PhD need GAT/subject test + interview.
📝
Entry Test
Merit-based (no general test); LAT for LLB; GAT for MS/PhD
👥
Seats
~3,000/year
🎓
Scholarships
HEC Need-Based, Ehsaas Undergraduate, PEEF, QAU Alumni scholarships, and Pakistan Bait-ul-Mal assistance.
🏠
Hostel
Extensive hostels for boys & girls (allotted on merit & distance basis)
