About IBA Karachi

Institute of Business Administration Karachi (IBA Karachi) is a public university in Karachi, recognised by the Higher Education Commission (HEC) of Pakistan, established in 1955.

Pakistan and South Asia's oldest business school, under the Sindh Government. A top corporate recruitment destination.

  • Oldest biz school in Asia
  • Strong corporate ties
  • Generous need-based aid
  • Harvard-affiliated curriculum
💰

Merit & Admission Criteria

Merit / eligibility: 85%+ marks, IBA test

Entry test: IBA Aptitude Test

Seats: about ~800/year.

Merit closes at a different level every year, depending on how many apply and how the test goes. Treat last year's figures as a guide, not a threshold.
