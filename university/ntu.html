About NTU

National Textile University (NTU) is a public university in Faisalabad, recognised by the Higher Education Commission (HEC) of Pakistan, established in 1959.

National Textile University (NTU) Faisalabad is Pakistan's only specialized textile university, established in 1959. It is the country's premier institution for textile engineering, design, and management education.

NTU Fee Structure 2026

Merit & Admission Criteria
