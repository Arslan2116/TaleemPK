About Aror

Aror University of Art, Architecture & Heritage is a public university in Sukkur, recognized by the Higher Education Commission (HEC) of Pakistan, established in 2013.

Quick Facts

💰
Fee / Semester
Semester fee (2026 official table): 1st semester Rs. 86,000; semesters 2–8: Rs. 60,250 each. 4-year BS programs total ~Rs. 5,07,750; BS Civil Engineering (5 years / 10 sems) total ~Rs. 6,28,250. Transport: city Rs. 10,000/sem, outstation Rs. 20,000/sem. Hostel Rs. 5,000/month.
🎯
Merit / Eligibility
CPN = 10% Matric + 20% Intermediate + 60% Entry Test + 10% Interview. Civil Engineering & Architecture: min 60% in HSC; all other programs: min 50%. Hafiz-e-Quran: +20 marks (with university Hifz test). Gap deduction: 1% per unaccounted gap year after SSC (max 3 years).
📝
Entry Test
Aror University Entry Test (paper-based, on campus). Civil Engineering: 100 Qs — English 30, Maths 30, Computer 10, Physics 10, Chemistry 10, GK 10. All other programs: 100 Qs — English 30, Maths 20, Computer 10, General Science 20, GK 20.
