About GIKI

Ghulam Ishaq Khan Institute of Engineering Sciences and Technology (GIKI) is a private university in Topi, Swabi, recognised by the Higher Education Commission (HEC) of Pakistan, established in 1993.

Ghulam Ishaq Khan Institute (GIKI) is one of Pakistan's most prestigious private engineering universities in Topi, KPK — known for rigorous academics, top facilities and fully residential campus life.

  • Top private engineering institute
  • Fully residential 400+ acre campus
  • Highly competitive merit
  • Strong global alumni in tech & engineering
💰

GIKI Fee Structure 2026

The fee at GIKI is approximately Rs. 4.1–4.7L/sem. Fees are revised each intake, so confirm the current figure with the university before you apply.

Undergraduate — Engineering & Computing (per sem)Rs. 470,000
Undergraduate — Management Sciences (per sem)Rs. 412,500
Graduate — MS / PhD tuition (per sem)Rs. 50,000
One-time Admission FeeRs. 75,000
Security Deposit (refundable)Rs. 40,000

Merit & Admission Criteria

Merit / eligibility: 85% GIKI Entry Test + 15% Matric. Eligibility: 60% overall and 60% in Maths/Physics (FSc/A-Level).

Entry test: GIKI Entry Test

Seats: about ~600/year.

Merit closes at a different level every year, depending on how many apply and how the test goes. Treat last year's figures as a guide, not a threshold.
