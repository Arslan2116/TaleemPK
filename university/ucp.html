About UCP

University of Central Punjab (UCP) is a private university in Lahore, recognised by the Higher Education Commission (HEC) of Pakistan, established in 2002.

University of Central Punjab (UCP) is a large private multidisciplinary university in Lahore (est. 2002), affiliated with Punjab Group of Colleges. Offers engineering, computing, business, pharmacy, law, media, languages, psychology and health sciences with a market-oriented academic model.

  • Large multidisciplinary private university
  • Strong CS, SE & Business programs
  • Punjab Group affiliation
  • Wide scholarship offerings (25–75%)
  • Industry-oriented curriculum

Merit & Admission Criteria

Merit / eligibility: UG: 50–60% in FSc/ICS/ICom/A-Level/DAE depending on program. Graduate: min CGPA 2.0–2.5. PhD: 3.0+ + interview.

Entry test: UCP Admission Test (or NTS / ECAT / LAT / MDCAT depending on program)

Seats: about ~5,000/year.

Merit closes at a different level every year, depending on how many apply and how the test goes. Treat last year's figures as a guide, not a threshold.
