About CUST

Capital University of Science & Technology (CUST) is a private university in Islamabad, recognised by the Higher Education Commission (HEC) of Pakistan, established in 1998.

Capital University of Science & Technology (CUST) is a private HEC-recognized university in Islamabad, known for strong engineering and IT programs with modern labs and industry linkages.
