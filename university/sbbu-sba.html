About SBBU-SBA

Shaheed Benazir Bhutto University, Shaheed Benazirabad (SBBU-SBA) is a public university in Shaheed Benazirabad, recognised by the Higher Education Commission (HEC) of Pakistan, established in 2012.

Public sector university in Shaheed Benazirabad (formerly Nawabshah), Sindh. Established in 2012 and recognized by HEC. Committed to providing quality, affordable higher education with one of the lowest tuition fees in Pakistan — Rs. 16,000/semester for undergraduate programs.

  • Public sector university – Government of Sindh (2012)
  • HEC recognized institution
  • Among lowest UG fees in Pakistan – Rs. 16,000/semester
  • Offers BS, MS, MPhil, LLB, and PhD programs
  • Located in Shaheed Benazirabad (Nawabshah), Sindh
💰

SBBU-SBA Fee Structure 2026

The fee at SBBU-SBA is approximately Rs. 16,000–91,000/sem. Fees are revised each intake, so confirm the current figure with the university before you apply.
