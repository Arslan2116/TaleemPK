About SIMS

Sindh Institute of Medical Sciences (SIMS) is a private university in Karachi, recognised by the Higher Education Commission (HEC) of Pakistan, established in 2009.

Sindh Institute of Medical Sciences (SIMS) is a degree-awarding institution established under the Sindh Act No. VII of 2009, recognized by HEC. Part of the prestigious SIUT (Sindh Institute of Urology and Transplantation), it offers completely free, high-quality education in medical and allied health sciences based solely on merit.

  • 100% free education (tuition-free)
  • HEC recognized degree-awarding institute
  • Part of SIUT – Pakistan's leading urology & transplant center
  • Established under Sindh Act No. VII of 2009
  • Merit-based admissions, free application process

Merit & Admission Criteria

Merit / eligibility: BS Nursing: min 60% in HSC Pre-Medical. Postgraduate: twice a year (January & August). Merit-based, non-discriminatory.

Entry test: Merit-based Admission Test + Interview

Merit closes at a different level every year, depending on how many apply and how the test goes. Treat last year's figures as a guide, not a threshold.
