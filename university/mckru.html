Merit / Eligibility
Undergraduate: Minimum 50% marks in Intermediate (FSc/FA/ICS/I.Com) or equivalent. LLB: FA/FSc/A-Level with at least 2nd division + valid LAT score from HEC. Postgraduate (MPhil): Bachelor's degree with min CGPA 2.5/4.0 and 50% marks in GAT (General). Result-awaiting candidates may apply.
