About NED University

NED University of Engineering and Technology (NED University) is a public university in Karachi / Tharparkar, recognised by the Higher Education Commission (HEC) of Pakistan, established in 1921.

NED University of Engineering & Technology is Pakistan's oldest engineering institution (est. 1921). Located in Karachi, it is a prestigious public university with a massive global alumni network and traditional engineering excellence.

  • Oldest engineering institution in Pakistan (1921)
  • Largest variety of engineering programs in Sindh
  • Massive global alumni network
  • Hosts NCAI & NCCS national centres
💰

NED University Fee Structure 2026

The fee at NED University is approximately Rs. 50–65K/sem. Fees are revised each intake, so confirm the current figure with the university before you apply.

Undergraduate — Regular Merit (per sem)Rs. 50,000 – 65,000
Undergraduate — Self-Finance (per year)Rs. 350,000 – 450,000+
Graduate — MS / PhD (per sem)Rs. 60,000 – 75,000
One-time Admission FeeRs. 15,000 – 20,000
📊

Merit & Admission Criteria

Merit / eligibility: 50% NED Entry Test + 50% HSC/A-Level. Eligibility: 60% in HSC Pre-Engineering/CS. Hafiz-e-Quran marks added.

Entry test: NED Entry Test

Seats: about ~3,000/year.

Merit closes at a different level every year, depending on how many apply and how the test goes. Treat last year's figures as a guide, not a threshold.
