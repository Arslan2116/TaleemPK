About Iqra

Iqra University (Iqra) is a private university in Karachi, recognised by the Higher Education Commission (HEC) of Pakistan, established in 1998.

Iqra University Karachi is a private university established in 1998 with campuses in Karachi, Islamabad, and Peshawar. It is known for business and IT programs with strong industry linkages.
