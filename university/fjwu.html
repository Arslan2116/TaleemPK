About FJWU

Fatima Jinnah Women University (FJWU) is a public university in Rawalpindi, recognised by the Higher Education Commission (HEC) of Pakistan, established in 1998.

Fatima Jinnah Women University (FJWU) Rawalpindi is a public women's university established in 1998, named after the Mother of the Nation. It provides quality higher education to women across Rawalpindi and surrounding areas.

FJWU Fee Structure 2026

The fee at FJWU is approximately Rs. 15–30K/sem. Fees are revised each intake, so confirm the current figure with the university before you apply.
