About Air University

Air University Islamabad (Air University) is a public university in Islamabad, recognised by the Higher Education Commission (HEC) of Pakistan, established in 2002.

Air University Islamabad is a public-sector university under the Pakistan Air Force (est. 2002), offering programs in engineering, computer science, aerospace/aviation, management and social sciences — with strong technical and defence-oriented education.

  • Pakistan Air Force affiliated
  • Strong CS & Engineering programs
  • Affordable vs private sector
  • Aerospace/Aviation focus
  • Industry-oriented technical education

Air University Fee Structure 2026

The fee at Air University is approximately Rs. 1.2–2.2L/sem. Fees are revised each intake, so confirm the current figure with the university before you apply.

Merit & Admission Criteria

Merit / eligibility: AU Entry Test + Matric/O-Level + FSc/A-Level weightage. Accepts FSc, A-Levels, equivalents.

Entry test: Air University Entry Test (or NTS for some)

Seats: about ~1,500/year.

Merit closes at a different level every year, depending on how many apply and how the test goes. Treat last year's figures as a guide, not a threshold.
