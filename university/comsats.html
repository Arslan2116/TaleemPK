About COMSATS

COMSATS University Islamabad (COMSATS) is a public university in Islamabad / Lahore / Wah / Attock / Sahiwal / Vehari / Abbottabad, recognised by the Higher Education Commission (HEC) of Pakistan, established in 1998.

COMSATS University Islamabad (CUI) is a top public-sector university known for IT, engineering and research, with seven campuses nationwide.

  • QS World Ranked
  • 7 campuses nationwide
  • Strong PhD faculty & research
  • Industry liaison & placements

COMSATS Fee Structure 2026

The fee at COMSATS is approximately Rs. 1.46–1.6L/sem. Fees are revised each intake, so confirm the current figure with the university before you apply.

Merit & Admission Criteria

Merit / eligibility: UG: 50% NTS NAT + 40% FSc + 10% Matric. MS: 40% GAT + 25% BS + 20% interview + 10% Inter + 5% Matric.

Entry test: NTS NAT (BS) / GAT General (MS) / GAT Subject (PhD)

Seats: about ~8,000/year.

Merit closes at a different level every year, depending on how many apply and how the test goes. Treat last year's figures as a guide, not a threshold.
