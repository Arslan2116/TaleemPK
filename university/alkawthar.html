About ALKAWTHAR

Merit / Eligibility
Admission Weightage: UPT (60%) + HSSC/Equivalent (25%) + SSC/Matric (15%). Minimum aggregate for admission: 50%. Eligibility: Minimum 45% marks in Intermediate (HSSC) or equivalent for most programs; BS Nursing requires FSc Pre-Medical with at least 50% marks. Admissions are offered twice a year (Spring and Fall semesters).
