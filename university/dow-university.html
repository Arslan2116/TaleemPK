About Dow University

Dow University of Health Sciences (Dow University) is a public university in Karachi, recognised by the Higher Education Commission (HEC) of Pakistan, established in 2004.

Karachi's largest medical university, affiliated with Dow Medical College (est. 1945) and backed by the Sindh Government.

  • Dow Medical College legacy
  • Largest med uni Karachi
  • Affordable
  • Sindh govt

Dow University Fee Structure 2026

The fee at Dow University is approximately Rs. 40–80K/sem. Fees are revised each intake, so confirm the current figure with the university before you apply.

📊

Merit & Admission Criteria

Merit / eligibility: MDCAT required, 70%+

Entry test: MDCAT

Seats: about ~1,200/year.

Merit closes at a different level every year, depending on how many apply and how the test goes. Treat last year's figures as a guide, not a threshold.
