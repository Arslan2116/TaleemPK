About NHU

Nazeer Hussain University (NHU) is a private university in Karachi, recognised by the Higher Education Commission (HEC) of Pakistan.

Private university chartered by the Government of Sindh and recognized by HEC. Offers programs across Architecture, Engineering, Business & Management, Pharmacy, and Rehabilitation & Health Sciences. Has 3,000+ registered students with 100 professional instructors.

  • Chartered by Government of Sindh
  • HEC recognized university
  • 3,000+ registered students
  • Programs in Architecture, Engineering, Business, Pharmacy, and Health Sciences
