About Habib University

Habib University is a private university in Karachi, recognised by the Higher Education Commission (HEC) of Pakistan, established in 2014.

Habib University is a private non-profit liberal arts and sciences university in Karachi (est. 2014), offering interdisciplinary undergraduate education with emphasis on critical thinking, design thinking and research-led learning.

  • Liberal Arts & Sciences model
  • 6 interdisciplinary UG degrees
  • 87% students receive financial aid
  • International faculty & curriculum
  • Strong critical thinking & communication focus

Merit & Admission Criteria

Merit / eligibility: Habib Entrance Test + interview + academic record + meta-curricular profile. SAT accepted (exemptions). Accepts A-Levels, IB, FBISE, AKU-EB, equivalents.

Entry test: Habib University Entrance Test (or SAT)

Seats: about ~400/year.

Merit closes at a different level every year, depending on how many apply and how the test goes. Treat last year's figures as a guide, not a threshold.
