About MUET

Mehran University of Engineering and Technology (MUET) is a public university in Jamshoro / Khairpur, recognised by the Higher Education Commission (HEC) of Pakistan, established in 1976.

Mehran University of Engineering and Technology (MUET), Jamshoro is one of Pakistan's leading public engineering universities — top engineering institution in Sindh with strong technical & research focus across engineering, computing, architecture and applied sciences.

  • Top public engineering university in Sindh
  • 200+ PhD faculty
  • Strong CS, AI & Engineering ecosystem
  • Industry partnerships & startup ecosystem
  • Very affordable public-sector fees

MUET Fee Structure 2026

The fee at MUET is approximately Rs. 42K–1.3L/sem. Fees are revised each intake, so confirm the current figure with the university before you apply.

Merit & Admission Criteria

Merit / eligibility: Engineering & BS: 60% in FSc Pre-Eng (or Pre-Med where applicable) / DAE / A-Level. Engineering Technology: 50%. Merit via MUET Computer-Based Pre-Admission Test.

Entry test: MUET Computer-Based Pre-Admission Test

Seats: about ~2,500/year.

Merit closes at a different level every year, depending on how many apply and how the test goes. Treat last year's figures as a guide, not a threshold.
